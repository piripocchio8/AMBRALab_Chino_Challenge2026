# Band `dual_lean` — ranks 16–20

The five highest-ipTM designs on the **M** orthologue among those not taken by `h_anchored`, ordered
by `boltz_iptm_M_mean` (0.443–0.530). Four of the five come from the two-hairpin family, the only
family declared against column 179 — Ser in H, Asn in M.

**None of these clears the threshold on H, and none clears it on M either.** They are here because
the brief asks for a dual binder and these carry the strongest M-side engagement the campaign
produced, not because they are shown to engage M. The threshold is calibrated on H-side controls
only; no molecule is known to bind the paralogue, so there is no positive control against which an M
number could be interpreted at all.

Ranks in this band are not comparable with ranks in `h_anchored`: the two bands are ordered on
different axes, one with a verified true positive behind it and one with none.
